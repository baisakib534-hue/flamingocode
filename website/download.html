Requirements

Desktop

  • Windows 10/11 (x64 or ARM64)
  • macOS 12+ (Apple Silicon or Intel)
  • Linux: glibc 2.31+ (Ubuntu 20.04+, Debian 11+, Fedora 35+, Arch)
  • 4 GB RAM minimum, 8 GB recommended
  • 2 GB disk space

Phone

  • Android 7.0 (API 24) or later
  • ARM64 architecture
  • Internet connection for relay pairing
  • Google account for sign-in

Verify Your Download

All releases are signed. Verify the SHA-256 checksums published in the release notes against the downloaded file:

# Windows
CertUtil -hashfile flamingocode-windows-x64.exe SHA256

# macOS
shasum -a 256 flamingocode-macos-arm64.dmg

# Linux
sha256sum flamingocode-linux-x64.AppImage

Public signing keys are published in the repository's docs/security.md.
