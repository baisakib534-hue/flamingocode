## H5.1 Scheduled tasks + watch loops (Hermes §17)
Schedule tasks in plain words or cron expressions — one-shot or
recurring, pause/resume/edit/trigger/remove, attach skills to a
job, deliver results to the chat; a NO-AGENT mode (plain script
on a schedule, zero LLM, stdout delivered verbatim); preflight
validation — a broken job alerts ONCE and makes NO LLM call (a
broken job never spends tokens); per-job model pin; a job bound
to a project directory (its rules load). Plus /loop-style WATCH
loops — "watch this build until it passes and ping me", fixed
interval or self-paced with change detection. This is the
feature that makes FlamingoCode work while he sleeps. Size M.
Web: yes (runs on the host/cloud); desktop: same.
